How it works

  • Sign in with GitHub. New accounts wait until an admin approves them.

  • Ask your AI to look around, find something, check a door or follow the cat.

The car has mecanum wheels, so it can drive sideways as well as forwards, and a camera at cat height. Every move is short and stops by itself, and one person drives at a time.
